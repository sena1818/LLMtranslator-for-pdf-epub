# The Buried Engine

Petroleum does not rest. Beneath the desert the buried life of the planet keeps moving, a slow black current that the Tellurian order can neither name nor stop. To read the desert as a surface is already a mistake; the surface is only the lid of a deeper machinery, and the machinery has its own appetite.

Hyperstition works here like a fever. A fiction is written, the desert answers, and the answer rewrites the fiction until no one can say which came first. The War Machine that crosses this ground is not an army but a tendency: it moves as Dust moves, without a center, refusing the striated map that the state lays over the buried life.

What the buried life wants, if it wants anything, is openness — not the openness of doors but the openness of the wound. Petroleum is the lubricant of that wound, and every pipeline is a small confession that the surface was never solid. The desert keeps its patience. Beneath the desert the engine turns, and the turning is older than the sun.
